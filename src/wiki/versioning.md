# Versioning your Layouts

As you build out your exhibition, you might want to try different arrangements without losing your current progress. CuraHub's Versioning system handles exactly this!

## Saving a Version
Think of a Version as a complete snapshot of where every piece of art is currently placed in your room.

1. Once you are happy with an arrangement, click the **Save Version** button.
2. Give your layout a descriptive name (e.g., "Minimalist Layout" or "Option B with extra lighting").
3. Your snapshot is now saved safely!

## Loading Previous Versions
If you decide you liked yesterday's arrangement better, you can easily go back:
1. Open the **Version Panel**.
2. Browse through your list of saved layouts.
3. Click **Load** on any historical layout to instantly restore that exact snapshot of the room.
